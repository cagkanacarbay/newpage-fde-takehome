02 Gate A: contradiction coverage

03 Gate B: entity recall

The corpus plants lookalike entities: rapamycin vs trametinib, dasatinib + quercetin in two different diseases, PhenoAge vs GrimAge vs DunedinPoAm. The gold paper must appear in the top 10 and rank above each named distractor. The confirmed threshold is 6 of 6. A distractor is a paper that matches the query’s vocabulary but names the wrong entity, so ranking it above the gold is a visible, deterministic failure.

C1 caveat

The hazard-ratio ranking itself appears only as a figure (Fig. 2A) and in Supplementary Table 8; it never appears as extractable table text. The two quotes above are the closest verbatim text anchors. If the parser drops figures, C1 passes on the abstract sentence alone, so the GrimAge sentence on page 10 is the part that actually discriminates.
